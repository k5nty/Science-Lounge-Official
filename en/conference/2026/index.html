National Letovo Engineers

8 April 2026 Letovo School, ground floor

The II “Letovo Engineers” conference: an annual science, engineering and IT conference for students in grades 8–11. Participants present engineering, science and IT projects to experts from leading Russian universities.

Three tracks: engineering, science, IT. Format: a poster session with product demonstrations for the engineering and IT tracks. Every participant gets a certificate, published abstracts and experience; winners get diplomas and the chance to publish articles in a peer-reviewed journal.
