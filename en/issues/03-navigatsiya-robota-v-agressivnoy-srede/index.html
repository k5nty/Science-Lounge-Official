How a robot finds its way in a hostile environment

13 March 2026, Fri, 16:30 Letovo School, S02

Imagine a robot in a hostile, unstructured environment. There is dust and dirt around, no road markings, and the GPS signal is either unavailable or jammed. How can it determine its position and keep moving? Andrey Zubov, a Letovo alumnus, will talk about this and much more.

SpeakerAndrey Zubov

Digital projects manager, Rusagro

Letovo School alumnus, digital projects manager at Rusagro, founder of an agrotech startup, student at MIPT and the Skolkovo business school.
