AI always, AI everywhere

18 September 2026, Fri, 16:10 Letovo School, lecture hall

How artificial intelligence is changing work, business and everyday life. How modern neural networks work, what their main weakness is, how a neural network gets to work with a computer and perform actions, what AI can do on an ordinary computer, why tokens and subscriptions exist, how AI is already used in business and what “AI agents”, “tools”, “harness” and “MCP servers” mean.

SpeakerIgor Gorshunov

Chief operating officer, AB Capital investment company

Chief operating officer of the investment company AB Capital. Spent twenty years as a finance director in companies of different industries. Today he rebuilds business processes with the help of AI.
