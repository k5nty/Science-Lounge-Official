About

2025

Science Lounge (“Nauchnye gostinye”) is a project by Letovo School students. We invite researchers and university teachers to speak to school students, and publish announcements, write-ups and recordings of the talks here.

Together with the lecture series we run the “Letovo Engineers” conference.
